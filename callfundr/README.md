# CallFundr for Claude

CallFundr is the complete home service operations platform: it answers every call, books and dispatches jobs, and keeps customers updated. This plugin connects Claude to your CallFundr account and adds skills for the jobs you do every day.

## What's included
- **Connector** — your CallFundr account in Claude (sign in with your CallFundr login; new users can create a 14-day free trial or try a sample shop).
- **Skills** — Morning rundown · Reshuffle the day · Chase payments · Maintenance season · End of day.

## How it works
Claude can only do what your CallFundr login can do. Anything that changes a job or contacts a customer shows a preview first and runs only on your go. Every request is logged in CallFundr under Settings → Claude.
