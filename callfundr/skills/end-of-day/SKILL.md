# End of day

1. Today: list_appointments for today. Count completed vs. not; list anything not finished with its reason if noted.
2. Money: list_payments for today and list_invoices still open from today's jobs.
3. Calls: list_calls and list_inquiries for today — anything that didn't turn into a job, so nothing is left hanging.
4. Tomorrow: the first jobs of the day and anything unassigned.

Five short sections, no fluff. Suggest at most one action for tonight (for example, "assign the two open jobs for tomorrow").
